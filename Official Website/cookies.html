The short version

This website sets no cookies of its own. If you decline advertising, nothing at all is stored about you beyond your own choice, and that choice lives in your browser's local storage rather than in a cookie. If you allow advertising, Google AdSense sets the cookies listed below, and you can change your mind at any time.

The Novaris application is a separate thing and is covered at the bottom of this page.

What this site uses

Local storage, not a cookie

When you answer the banner, the site records your answer under the key novaris.consent.v1 in your browser's local storage. Local storage is not a cookie: it is not sent to us with requests, and it cannot be read by other sites. It exists so you are not asked the same question on every page, and so that declining is as easy as accepting.

Advertising cookies, only if you allow them

The advertising script is not added to the page at all until you allow it. On a refusal, the request to Google is never made, so no Google cookie is set and no identifier is created for your browser. If you later change your answer to a refusal, the script is removed from the page as well.

Changing your mind

Use the Change your choice link at the bottom of any page. It clears your stored decision and brings the banner back immediately. Declining then removes the advertising script and refuses to reload it.

To stop personalised advertising regardless of this site:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • aboutads.info choices
  • youronlinechoices.com for the wider EU/UK framework

The application

The application is separate and does not use cookies for anything you would recognise. Its state lives in your Windows profile as ordinary files. If you enable the clear-on-exit option, cookies and site storage are deleted from the browser's profile when you close it normally, which is a deletion rather than a cookie setting.

Private windows use storage that is never written to disk in the first place.

Managing cookies in your browser

Every major browser can delete cookies for a single site or entirely. Blocking third-party cookies for this site is a reasonable setting if you would rather ads did not work at all, and it does not affect anything else on the page.

If you block or delete cookies, the banner will ask again next time, because the consent record itself lives in local storage and is cleared alongside them in most browsers. Nothing breaks; the site simply asks once more.
