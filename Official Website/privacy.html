The two things to keep apart

This statement covers two separate systems, and conflating them is the most common way a privacy policy misleads people. Read them apart.

  • The Novaris application is the software you install. It has no account system and no server to talk to. It does not send telemetry, and it does not contact Google or any other analytics provider.
  • This website carries advertising so the project can be funded. When you allow it, Google AdSense is loaded and may set cookies. That is a deliberate, disclosed choice, made here and not in the application.

So: if you install Novaris and browse with it, nothing you visit is reported to anyone. If you read this website and allow advertising, the advertising partner receives the information described below, and not before you say yes.

What the application does

Novaris stores everything it needs in your own Windows user profile folder. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server component, which means there is nowhere for your browsing to be uploaded to even in principle.

Browsing data

History, bookmarks, tab groups, workspaces and your reading list are written to %APPDATA%/Novaris Browser/novaris-data.json on your own disk. This is deleted when you uninstall, and it is never transmitted. If you turn on the optional clear-on-exit setting, it is erased each time you close the browser normally.

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting

There is no event reporting of any kind. We did not build it, so there is no switch to turn off, because there is nothing to turn off.

We have also verified this rather than assuming it. A Chromium network log recorded over a real session, visiting three unrelated sites, produced 39 requests to exactly three hosts — all of them page content. There were no Safe Browsing pings, no component updater calls, no variations service requests, no autofill upload and no telemetry. Chromium ships with those services disabled and Novaris does not re-enable them.

Phishing checks and the Trust Card

The Website Trust Card scores a site's address to decide whether it imitates a brand you trust. That analysis is performed entirely on your PC. No address is submitted to us or to anyone else, and the check works with no internet connection at all.

Ad blocking

Requests are blocked on your machine before they leave it. Because the request never goes out, the destination never learns that you visited a page that contained it.

Tab transfer

Moving a tab to another device connects the two devices directly on your local network. There is no relay, no server and no cloud copy. The tab is encrypted on the sending device before it moves, and the sending device deletes its copy once the transfer budget is used.

Private windows

A private window uses a storage partition that is never written to disk and is released when the window closes. Nothing about the session survives, and the window has no access to the vault or to the profile's stored history.

Your passwords

Novaris never reads a Chrome or Edge password database on its own. Importing is something you start, and you can see exactly what will be imported before it is saved.

Once saved, each credential is sealed individually using AES-256-GCM with a random key and nonce of its own, under a key derived from your browser password using Argon2id. The records are additionally protected by Windows' own encryption at rest. A credential is only ever filled back into the exact host it was saved for, so a lookalike sign-in page cannot collect it.

We cannot recover your vault. Nobody can, including us. If you lose your browser password, the only supported route is to reset the vault, which destroys its contents. This is deliberate: a support process that can reset a vault is a support process that can open one.

What this website collects

This is the part that differs from the application, so it is set out in full.

If you decline advertising

No advertising script is requested from Google, no advertising cookie is set, and no Google identifier is created for your browser. The site is static files and one small script. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is never transmitted.

If you allow advertising

Google AdSense is loaded, and advertising and measurement cookies may be set, including:

  • IDE, DSID, RUL and _gcl_au, which are Google's advertising identifiers.
  • test_cookie, used to check that your browser accepts cookies.
  • Google's NID and similar preference cookies.
  • Third-party cookies set by any other advertising partner in the network.

These are used to measure whether an advert is shown, to limit how often you see the same one, and — where you have not opted out of personalisation — to select advertising based on your activity. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info. Do that before or after allowing ads here; both work.

Server logs

Whoever hosts this site keeps ordinary web server logs, which typically include the requested address, the time, and a truncated network address. These are used to keep the site working and are not combined with advertising profiles.

What we do not do on this website

No first-party analytics. No advertising pixels in the application. No selling of anything.

Your rights

You can change your advertising choice at any time using the Change your choice link in the footer, which clears your stored decision and brings the banner back. Declining afterwards removes the advertising script from the page as well as declining to add it.

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Because the application stores everything locally and we hold no copy of it, there is generally nothing for us to hand over. For data held by the advertising partner, the exercise of those rights is best directed at them, and the links above are the quickest route.

Children

Novaris is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The application creates no online account, so there is no profile to have.

Contact and changes

If anything here is unclear or you believe it is inaccurate, say so. A privacy statement that cannot be questioned is worth very little.

When this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is described in the release notes shipped inside the application.
